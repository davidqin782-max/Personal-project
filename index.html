What I’m about

I like getting to the heart of a problem, then finding a clear way forward.

I’m David, a computer science student with a strong interest in mathematics, analytical thinking, and the systems behind the technology we use every day. Math has taught me to be patient with difficult questions, look for structure in complexity, and stay excited by elegant solutions.

Through coursework and independent learning, I’m building a foundation in programming, algorithms, data structures, and web development. I enjoy breaking large problems into smaller steps, learning unfamiliar tools, and turning abstract ideas into practical, understandable results.

I’m also developing the work habits that make a strong teammate: communicating clearly, asking thoughtful questions, documenting my process, and incorporating feedback. I’m looking for opportunities where I can contribute a careful, curious perspective while continuing to grow alongside experienced engineers and collaborators.
